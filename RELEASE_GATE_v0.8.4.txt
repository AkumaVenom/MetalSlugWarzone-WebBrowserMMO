COMPETITIVE AI SCOPE
- Existing WarzoneAI rows keep their identity, history, resources, staff and FOB placement.
- A one-time state-preserving activation marks the existing population overdue so the new catch-up model begins on normal requests without a Fresh Install.
- Normal action cadence is 4–11 seconds before stable rival-class/personality pacing.
- Request-driven simulation keeps the existing per-bot lease and next_action_at serialization; elapsed downtime is converted into bounded compact catch-up instead of being discarded.
- Productive activity dominates: field combat/recovery, Mother Base development/training, base management, Dispatch, staff FOB operations, direct FOB operations and PvP. Failed/unavailable competitive actions fall back to productive development or field work.
- Competitive Base Power targets follow the stronger of a 3,500 floor or the current strongest human Base Power.
- The 1,000 durable bot indexes deterministically resolve to 726 Active Rivals, 202 Contenders, 65 Elite Rivals and 7 Apex Rivals.
- Elite/Apex rarity is deliberate. Their higher staff quality, larger roster ceilings, faster cadence and stronger development targets permit a small high-power tail rather than making all bots identical.
- Base Power remains derived from real owned staff, real assignments and the established sector score × sector level formula. The bot runtime contains no arbitrary competitive Base Power setter.
- Human FOB target preference remains 28%; existing FOB protection, transfer limits, Dispatch settlement and PvP/FOB authority are unchanged.

PERFORMANCE / REGRESSION SAFEGUARDS
- Bot staff assignment uses batched sector/capacity snapshots rather than repeated per-sector capacity queries.
- Bot Base Power recalculation uses one grouped staff aggregate and one multi-row sector upsert, while preserving the established calculation formula.
- Catch-up work is capped and compact; it does not start one PHP worker per commander or loop through every missed historical action.
- AI Network Rival Class is integrated inside the existing Commander cell rather than adding a wider table column.
- CSS, JavaScript and runtime image files are intentionally untouched to preserve the accepted v0.8.3 sizing, artwork and facing behavior.
